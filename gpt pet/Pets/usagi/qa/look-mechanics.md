# Usagi gaze mechanics
Feet and lower torso remain planted front-facing at fixed size and ground baseline. The large rounded head pitches up/down and gently yaws left/right; the ears follow the head subtly but remain attached. Preserve original tiny dark-brown glossy eyes with white highlights: do not add sclera or replacement pupils. Move the entire facial feature group with the head turn; nose/mouth and eye pair shift together on the face surface. For up the mouth/nose and eyes sit high on the round head with a raised chin and shortened forehead. For down the facial group sits low under a broad forehead with lowered chin. For right/left the face group and nose tip move toward the respective screen edge, while the far eye foreshortens. Keep both eyes visible and expression recognizable at all orientations; this is a head gaze sweep, not a body turntable. Diagonals combine horizontal yaw and vertical pitch, smoothly and monotonically. Keep ears within bounds and face landmarks unmistakable at native size.
